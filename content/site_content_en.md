# Short Bio

Haodan Tang is a Lecturer and Master's Supervisor at the International Business School, Southwestern University of Finance and Economics. He received his Ph.D. in World Economy from Nankai University and was a joint Ph.D. student at the Dyson School of Applied Economics and Management, Cornell University. His research interests include the digital economy, international investment, innovation economics, technology finance, and industrial economics.
